My Dearest Randy,

If this letter had been written a hundred years ago, I would have sealed it with red wax and hidden it somewhere inside the oldest room of this castle, waiting for you to find it.

I still cannot believe how lucky I was that our paths crossed. Somehow, you became a beautiful part of my life — something I never planned for, but never want to lose.

You, the werewolf. Me, the vampire. An impossible little story living inside an ancient castle.

And maybe that is exactly why I love it. Because if eternity were real, I would still choose you.

I remember the enormous old castle you once dreamed about: endless halls, ancient windows, cold moonlight, and two people who keep choosing each other, night after night.

I hope one day that fantasy becomes something more than a dream. I hope the castle becomes a home, the story becomes our life, and the promise becomes marriage — not for a night, but for as long as we are given.

I treasure the closeness we have shared, the private moments that belong only to us, and every little piece of intimacy that made our bond feel real.

You are one of the rare people I feel incredibly lucky to have found.

Happy Birthday, Randy. May the night be kind to you, and may I always find my way back to you.

Forever yours,
